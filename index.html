From static images to a self-serve email system

Every email was a flat image. A typo meant a new design request. Localising a campaign took weeks. Led the shift to a coded, modular MJML template system built to survive Outlook, Gmail, and dark mode — not just look good on the designer's screen.

  • Turnaround dropped from days to minutes — localised versions from weeks to minutes.
  • Removed the designer bottleneck for most sends; one set of core assets could generate 40+ campaign variations without further design input.
  • Doubled recommended products in transactional emails via a custom two-column block, at zero extra length.
  • Fixed mobile rendering, accessibility, and cross-client bugs — including a shared component that let the same template run on both the core ESP and Microsoft Dynamics.

case study 02 / 03
role  analytics & attribution
stack ga4 · gtm · looker studio · utm
scope 20-clinic network

Replacing a misleading percentage with real numbers

After an acquisition, marketing "success" was judged by a patchwork of conflicting signals — a booking-split percentage that hid seasonal swings, call centre gut feeling, and agency reports built to flatter their own channel. Built UTM tagging and two Looker Studio dashboards to replace opinion with a real, comparable picture.

  • Proved budget was cannibalising organic search — cut paid spend with confidence and watched organic pick up the difference.
  • Right-sized effort across channels — organic and paid made up the largest share of results despite disproportionate internal focus on email and SMS.
  • Culled a resource-intensive SMS campaign costing real weekly budget and staff time for roughly one conversion a month.
  • Turned “this campaign did nothing" disputes into data-backed conversations, campaign by campaign.
honest limitation · utm tracking
browser privacy changes and tracking prevention can strip or block utm parameters
treated as a strong directional signal, not a perfectly complete count — and said so, rather than overstating precision
tools → GA4 · Google Tag Manager · Looker Studio · UTM parameters
case study 03 / 03
role  lifecycle segmentation
stack dynamics · email · sms · loyalty data
result automated + cheaper, same outcome

Testing whether the discount actually mattered

A manual monthly win-back call list worked well but ate real time from both marketing and the call centre. Automated it into a segmented, multi-channel journey — then used the automation to test an assumption nobody had questioned: did the discount actually need to be that big?

  • Automated a manual monthly process, sequencing email → SMS → call so the call centre only worked the harder remaining cases.
  • Roughly doubled SMS click-through among high-loyalty clients by segmenting the send instead of treating the list as one audience.
  • Cut the reactivation discount substantially across two rounds of testing, with only a marginal effect on conversion.
  • Reduced call centre volume by routing easier conversions through cheaper, automated channels first.
measurement — an honest note Attribution here was genuinely messy: dashboard data, manually-pulled CRM segments, and self-reported call outcomes never fully reconciled. As the journey improved and the call list got shorter, call centre motivation to finish it quietly dropped too — a good problem, but one that still muddies a clean before/after read.
